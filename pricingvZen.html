/Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

A few things people usually ask before starting a conversation.

Which model is right for us?

If you have one specific question to answer — can this be funded, will this market accept it, does this prototype hold up — a Pilot Engagement is usually the right size. If you already know you need sustained support across several fronts, the Full Partnership fits better. If you're pre-revenue with a strong lab-stage technology, ask about the Equity Partnership.

Do you ever work without any fee?

Yes — our Lab Validation service can run entirely at our own expense in exchange for an equity stake, when we believe in the opportunity. This is decided case by case, not offered automatically.

How is a project actually scoped and priced?

We start with a short conversation about your technology, current stage, and the specific challenge you're facing. From there we propose a scope of work and a structure — project fee, retainer, or equity — together with you, before anything is agreed.

Can a Pilot Engagement lead into a longer partnership?

Often, yes. Many of our longer engagements started as a single scoped deliverable. There's no obligation either way — the pilot's findings are yours to use regardless of what comes next.
